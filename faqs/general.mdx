Find answers to the most common questions about Dimedove.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Dimedove?">
    Dimedove is a customer engagement platform built on agents that qualify leads and deliver personalized experiences for you. Configure your agents to match your brand, deploy them across multiple channels, and manage all conversations from a unified inbox.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What channels does Dimedove support?">
    Dimedove supports a website messenger widget, standalone forms, email, telephony (voice and SMS), Slack, and social channels including Facebook Messenger, Instagram, and TikTok. You can also build your own experience on top of the [Apps API](../developer/apps/overview). All conversations across every channel are managed from a single unified inbox.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I customize my agent's behavior and personality?">
    Yes. You can configure your agent's identity, instructions, personality, tone, first message, verbosity, human fallback behavior, and more. For rules that only apply in specific situations, use [Procedures](../platform/agents/procedures) instead of adding everything to your instructions. See our [Agent Configuration](../platform/agents/overview) guide for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What integrations are available?">
    Quite a few. For records and CRM, Dimedove connects to HubSpot, Salesforce, Attio, Pipedrive, Close, Zoho CRM, Airtable, and Applied Epic. For scheduling, Calendly, Cal.com, Acuity Scheduling, and Outlook Calendar. Slack, Facebook Messenger, and Instagram are available as channels, and Notion, Linear, Intercom, Zendesk, and Shopify are coming soon. Check the [Integrations](../integrations/slack) tab for the latest.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Dimedove have an API?">
    Yes. The [Apps API](../developer/apps/overview) lets you build fully custom experiences on top of your agents: create conversations, send messages, and receive agent responses with streaming or traditional request and response. A completions endpoint is also available for one-shot generation with no conversation, such as autocompleting a field or drafting a message. We support [webhooks](../developer/webhooks/overview) for real-time event notifications and [MCP servers](../developer/mcp/overview) for connecting agents to external AI tools. See the [API Reference](/api-reference/overview) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I install the agent on my website?">
    Add the JavaScript widget code to your website using your agent ID and publishable key. See our [Installation Guide](../platform/agents/install) for step-by-step instructions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do workflows work?">
    Workflows let you connect platform events to automated actions. Configure triggers (like a new conversation starting, a contact being created or updated, or an inbound call) and define the actions that follow, from sending a message to running one of your agent's tasks or enriching a contact. See the [Workflows](../platform/workflows/overview) documentation to get started.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can my agents reach out first, or only respond?">
    Both. Alongside answering inbound conversations, your agents can place outbound calls and send outbound SMS, either one contact at a time from a workflow or as a batch you upload and schedule. Every outbound conversation lands in your Inbox like any other. See [Outbound](../platform/telephony/outbound) for setup and the compliance considerations.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can my agents book meetings on my calendar?">
    Yes. Connect Calendly, Cal.com, Acuity Scheduling, or Outlook Calendar, and your agent can check real availability, book, and reschedule inside the conversation rather than handing over a link. Visitors on chat and forms pick a time from an interactive widget, and callers hear the same slots read out. See [Calendar tasks](../platform/agents/tasks/calendar).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What are credits, and how do they differ from Qualified Opportunities?">
    They measure different things. A Qualified Opportunity reflects a meaningful outcome in a conversation. Credits measure the volume of routine, high-frequency work your agents do, such as moderating and replying to social comments. Both are included in your plan and billed separately, and every credit-consuming action is recorded so you can trace your usage. See [Credits](../platform/team/credits).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What is Ask Dimedove?">
    Ask Dimedove is a copilot over your own workspace. You can ask it about your conversations, contacts, and agents, group and chart your data, and have it propose configuration changes. It never applies a change on its own: every proposal arrives as a before and after comparison you approve or reject, and approved agent changes land as a draft until you publish. See [Ask Dimedove](../platform/ask-dimedove/overview).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I contact support?">
    For support questions, reach out to support@dimedove.com. If you need onboarding help or want to speak with the founder directly, [book a call with Felix](https://cal.com/felixsimard/onboarding).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
