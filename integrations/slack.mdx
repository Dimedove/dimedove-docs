Dimedove's Slack integration enables powerful connections between your Slack workspace and your AI agents. Connect your workspace to deploy agents directly to Slack channels and receive real-time notifications about important events.

## Integration Features

### Slack Agents

Deploy your Dimedove agents to Slack channels where team members can interact with them directly. Agents respond in threads, keeping conversations organized while providing consistent agent responses.

### Key Capabilities

- **Channel Deployment**: Add agents to public and private channels
- **Mention-Based Triggers**: Configure agents to respond only when @mentioned, or to every reply in the channel's threads
- **Threaded Conversations**: All responses organized in threads for clean channel management
- **Files and Images**: Agents read the images and documents shared in the conversation
- **Ask Dimedove**: A direct message with the Dimedove app, or a channel set up with `/dimedove add ask`, reaches your team's own assistant, with approvals right in the thread
- **Unified Inbox**: Conversations with your Slack agents appear alongside other channels in your Dimedove dashboard

## Getting Connected

To connect your Slack workspace:

1. Navigate to **Settings > Integrations** in your Dimedove dashboard
2. Click **Configure** next to Slack
3. Authorize Dimedove in your Slack workspace
4. Start creating Slack Agents and deploying them to channels

<Info>Slack integration requires a Pro subscription.</Info>
