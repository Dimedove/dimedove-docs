Dimedove's TikTok integration connects your TikTok business account to your AI agents. Automate responses to direct messages and take care of the comments on your videos, all from your Dimedove dashboard.

## Integration Features

### Direct Message Automation

Link your TikTok business account to a Dimedove agent that answers incoming direct messages automatically, with the same knowledge and personality it uses on every channel.

### Comment Interactions

Your agents can engage with comments on your videos:

- **Public Replies**: Respond to comments directly on the video with short, to-the-point answers
- **Comment Moderation**: Hide comments that match the rules you describe, and bring them back when needed

### Key Capabilities

- **Automated DM Responses**: AI agents respond to incoming direct messages in real time
- **Unified Inbox**: All TikTok conversations appear alongside your other channels in your Dimedove dashboard
- **Contact Tracking**: TikTok contacts are created automatically for a complete view of each customer
- **Comment Activity**: See every comment your agent handled, what it did, and why

## Getting Connected

To connect your TikTok account:

1. Navigate to **Settings > Integrations** in your Dimedove dashboard
2. In the **Social** group, click **Configure** on **TikTok**
3. Authorize Dimedove with your TikTok business account
4. Under **Socials**, create a channel for the account and assign its agent. See [TikTok](/platform/socials/tiktok)

<Info>TikTok integration requires a Pro subscription with the Social Channels feature enabled.</Info>
