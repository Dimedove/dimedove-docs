Applied Epic is an insurance agency management system (AMS) by Applied Systems, used by independent agencies and brokerages as their system of record for clients, policies, and day-to-day servicing work. Dimedove's Applied Epic integration connects your AI agents to your Epic database so they can look up records and write back the outcomes of a conversation, such as logging an activity against a client.

## Integration features

### Supported resources

Once connected, Dimedove discovers the resources available in your Epic database, including:

- **Clients**: the core customer/account records.
- **Contacts**: the people associated with clients, prospects, carriers, and vendors.
- **Activities**: the logged tasks, follow-ups, and servicing notes tied to a client.
- **Companies, opportunities, policies, and accounts**: additional records your agents can read.

### Agent actions

Configure your Dimedove agents to perform record operations as part of their tasks. For each task you choose the operation the agent is allowed to perform:

- **Get**: look up a record by fields such as email, phone, or ID (for example, find a client before logging work against it).
- **Create**: create a new record, such as an activity logging a call outcome.
- **Update**: update an existing record with information gathered during the conversation.
- **Upsert**: look up a record and update it, or create one if no match is found.

A common pattern is a two-step sequence: the agent first looks up the client, then creates an activity against that client using the returned client ID.

### Key capabilities

- **Per-agency connection**: connect your own Epic database using credentials issued to your agency.
- **Field mapping**: map agent conversation outputs to specific Epic fields for precise data entry.
- **Unified inbox**: conversations that trigger Applied Epic actions appear alongside all other channels in your Dimedove dashboard.

## Getting connected

Applied Epic connects with API credentials rather than an OAuth sign-in. You provision an application in the Applied Dev Center, which issues a consumer key and consumer secret scoped to your Epic database, then enter those credentials in Dimedove.

To connect your Applied Epic database:

1. Provision an application in the [Applied Dev Center](https://devcenter.myappliedproducts.com/home) to obtain a consumer key and consumer secret.
2. Navigate to **Settings > Integrations** in your Dimedove dashboard.
3. Click **Configure** next to Applied Epic.
4. Enter your **consumer key**, **consumer secret**, and **database name**.
5. Click **Save and Connect**. Dimedove verifies the credentials and discovers your available resources.
6. Configure agent actions to start looking up records and logging activities.

<Info>Applied Epic integration requires a Pro subscription with third-party integrations enabled.</Info>
