## Overview

Where Dimedove Technologies Inc. ("Dimedove") processes personal information on a Business Customer's behalf, it acts as a data processor and engages certain subprocessors that may process personal data submitted to Dimedove's AI agent building platform and related services. Dimedove acts in its own capacity for account administration, billing, security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, and its own business operations, as described in Section 13.3 of our [Privacy Policy](https://docs.dimedove.com/legal/privacy-policy). These subprocessors are listed below with a description of their services, the data they may access, and data processing locations.

Dimedove services and data are hosted in the Amazon Web Services (AWS) **Canada Central (ca-central-1)** region. This list may be updated by Dimedove from time to time in accordance with our customer contracts.

---

## Core Subprocessors (Always Active)

These subprocessors are integral to the operation of the Dimedove platform and process data for all customers.

**Amazon Web Services, Inc.**: Cloud hosting, storage, and processing (including AI processing) of customer data. Accesses all customer data as the infrastructure provider. (Canada)

**Stripe, Inc.**: Payment processing, subscription billing, and financial transaction services. Accesses billing email, payment method details, and usage meter data. (USA)

**Kinde Pty Ltd**: User authentication, identity management, and access control services. Accesses authentication credentials, user identity information, and session data. (Australia/Global)

**Resend, Inc.**: Transactional email delivery, including emails that AI agents and workflows send to end users. Accesses recipient email addresses and email content. (USA)

**Loops Technologies, Inc.**: Email delivery for platform notifications and marketing. Accesses team member email addresses, notification content, and marketing preferences. (USA)

**Temporal Technologies Inc.**: Workflow orchestration for automations, outbound batches, and agent work. Accesses record identifiers and timestamps only, not message content. (Canada)

**Intercom, Inc.**: Customer support messenger in the Dimedove dashboard. Accesses team member names, email addresses, and support conversations. (USA)

## AI Model Providers

These subprocessors provide AI inference capabilities. Dimedove selects the AI models and providers used to deliver the Services.

**OpenAI, L.L.C.**: AI model services and natural language processing. Accesses conversation content transmitted for AI inference. (USA)

**Google L.L.C.**: AI processing (Gemini models), including reading images and documents shared in conversations, website analytics, and OAuth sign-in. Accesses conversation content and attachments for AI inference, and authentication data. (USA/Global)

**Anthropic, PBC**: AI model services and processing. Accesses conversation content transmitted for AI inference. (USA)

**Groq, Inc.**: High-performance AI inference and processing services. Accesses conversation content transmitted for AI inference. (USA)

**Cerebras Systems Inc.**: High-performance AI inference used to process agent tool results. Accesses conversation context and tool results transmitted for AI inference. (USA)

**Baseten, Inc.**: AI model hosting for phone-based AI agents. Accesses call transcripts and context transmitted for AI inference. (USA)

**TypeSafe**: Review of messages drafted by AI agents before they are sent. Accesses message drafts and related context, with personal identifiers masked before transmission. (USA)
