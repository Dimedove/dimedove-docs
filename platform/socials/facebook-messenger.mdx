<Info>Facebook Messenger is available to teams on the Pro subscription.</Info>

Deploy your Dimedove agents to your Facebook Pages. Your agent answers the people who message your Page on Messenger, and takes care of the comments on your Page's posts. In the dashboard, the platform appears as **Messenger**.

## Prerequisites

- A Dimedove account with the Pro subscription
- A Facebook Page you manage
- At least one Dimedove agent that is published and live

## Setup

### Step 1: Connect Your Facebook Pages

1. Navigate to **Settings > Integrations** in your Dimedove dashboard
2. In the **Social** group, click **Configure** on **Messenger**
3. Sign in to Facebook, choose the Pages you want to connect, and click **Allow**

To add more Pages later, click **Manage** on the card, then **Reconnect**, and update your selection.

### Step 2: Create a Social Channel

1. Go to **Socials**, under Channels in your dashboard sidebar, and click **Add**
2. Give the channel a **Name**, choose the **Assigned Agent**, and set the **Channel** to **Messenger**
3. Select the **Connected Business Page** this channel responds to

### Step 3: Choose What Your Agent Handles

- **Direct Messages**: your agent answers the Messenger conversations people start with your Page
- **Comments & Moderation**: your agent handles comments on your Page's posts. Turn on **Public reply** for it to answer in the thread, add optional **Reply Instructions**, and turn on **Auto-hide Comments** with a description of which comments to hide

Click **Save**, then turn the channel on.

## What Your Agent Does

- **Answers Messenger conversations** with the same knowledge and personality it uses on every channel, reading the images and files people share
- **Replies to comments** on your posts, hides the ones that match your rules, and lets the rest be
- **Keeps you in the loop**: every conversation appears in your Dimedove [Inbox](/platform/inbox/overview), contacts are created automatically, and the channel's **Activity** tab shows every comment it handled

See the [Socials overview](/platform/socials/overview) for how comment moderation, credits, and reconnecting work across platforms.
