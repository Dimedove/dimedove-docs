<Info>TikTok is available to teams on the Pro subscription.</Info>

Deploy your Dimedove agents to TikTok. Your agent answers the direct messages people send your business account, and takes care of the comments on your videos.

## Prerequisites

- A Dimedove account with the Pro subscription
- A TikTok business account
- At least one Dimedove agent that is published and live

## Setup

### Step 1: Connect Your TikTok Account

1. Navigate to **Settings > Integrations** in your Dimedove dashboard
2. In the **Social** group, click **Configure** on **TikTok**
3. Sign in with your TikTok business account and allow Dimedove

### Step 2: Create a Social Channel

1. Go to **Socials**, under Channels in your dashboard sidebar, and click **Add**
2. Give the channel a **Name**, choose the **Assigned Agent**, and set the **Channel** to **TikTok**
3. Select the **Connected TikTok Account** this channel responds to

### Step 3: Choose What Your Agent Handles

- **Direct Messages**: your agent answers the direct messages people send your account
- **Comments & Moderation**: your agent handles comments on your videos. Turn on **Public reply** for it to answer in the thread, add optional **Reply Instructions**, and turn on **Auto-hide Comments** with a description of which comments to hide

Click **Save**, then turn the channel on.

<Tip>TikTok keeps comment replies short, so your agent's public replies are brief. Ask for concise answers in your **Reply Instructions** to make every word count.</Tip>

## What Your Agent Does

- **Answers direct messages** with the same knowledge and personality it uses on every channel
- **Replies to comments** on your videos, hides the ones that match your rules, and lets the rest be
- **Keeps you in the loop**: every conversation appears in your Dimedove [Inbox](/platform/inbox/overview), contacts are created automatically, and the channel's **Activity** tab shows every comment it handled

See the [Socials overview](/platform/socials/overview) for how comment moderation, credits, and reconnecting work across platforms.
