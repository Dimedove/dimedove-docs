# What is Dimedove?

Dimedove is a customer engagement platform built on agents that qualify leads and engage customers for you. Deploy agents across multiple channels, manage all conversations from a unified inbox, and automate workflows, all while maintaining the human touch your customers value.

## Built for the AI Era

As businesses adapt to an AI-first world, customer expectations are evolving. Dimedove empowers you to create intelligent agents that provide instant, personalized interactions at scale.

## Key Capabilities

- **Intelligent Agent Builder**: Create sophisticated AI agents with custom identity, knowledge, and behavior
- **Multi-Channel Engagement**: Deploy across your website messenger, forms, email, phone, Slack, Microsoft Teams, and social media
- **Lead Qualification**: Automatically identify and qualify prospects through conversations
- **Goals**: Give an agent an outcome to reach with a contact, and let it plan, follow up, and report back over days, with your approval where you want it
- **Unified Inbox**: Manage all conversations across channels in one place
- **Workflow Automation**: Build branching workflows that connect platform events to automated actions
- **Ask Dimedove**: Query your workspace and delegate work in plain language, from the dashboard, Slack, Microsoft Teams, or email
- **Developer Tools**: REST API, webhooks, and MCP servers for custom integrations
