Ask Dimedove is your internal copilot: a conversational assistant that lets your team work across your entire Dimedove workspace using plain language. Ask your copilot to find and explain anything in your workspace, draft changes for you to approve, and hand work to your agents. Search and analyze your data, understand how your agents are configured, and propose changes to those configurations, all from a single chat.

The goal is to make iterating on your workspace seamless. Instead of clicking through screens to find a conversation, check a setting, or rewrite an agent's instructions, you describe what you want and Ask Dimedove does the work with you.

## What Ask Dimedove Can Do

### Query Your Data
- Search across all your conversations by topic, channel, contact, or outcome
- Look up contacts and the insights collected about them
- Read full conversation transcripts when you need the details

### Understand Usage Patterns
- Count and group conversations by channel, time period, or status
- Surface trends across your workspace, such as what happened this week
- Review how often conversations needed a human or turned into qualified opportunities

### Inspect Agent Configurations
- Review how any agent is set up, including its instructions, tone, and personality
- See your channels across forms, email, Slack, social, and phone or SMS
- Check social comment moderation settings and recent comment activity

### Modify and Manage Configurations
- Rewrite an agent's instructions or adjust its tone and personality
- Propose configuration changes for review before anything goes live
- Update social channel comment instructions

### Delegate Work
- Start a [goal](/platform/goals/overview) for a contact and let an agent follow through over days
- Ask one of your agents what it would tell a customer, answered from its published configuration and knowledge
- Have an agent run one of its tools, such as a CRM record lookup or sending an email
- Work from the dashboard, Slack, Microsoft Teams, or email

See [Delegating work](/platform/ask-dimedove/delegation) for how delegation and approvals work outside the dashboard.

<Info>
  Changes to your configurations are always proposed for your review first. Ask Dimedove never applies a change to a live agent or channel without your explicit approval. See [Usage](/platform/ask-dimedove/how-to) for how the review and approval flow works.
</Info>

## How You Access It

Ask Dimedove is available throughout the platform so you can ask a question wherever you are:

- **Home page**: A prompt box at the center of your dashboard where you can start a new conversation, with files attached if you need them
- **Top navigation**: An Ask Dimedove button in the page header, available on most screens, also opened with **⌘K** (**Ctrl+K** on Windows)
- **Full chat view**: A dedicated, expanded view for longer conversations and follow-ups
- **Slack, Microsoft Teams, and email**: Your teammates can reach Ask Dimedove without opening the dashboard. See [Delegating work](/platform/ask-dimedove/delegation).

You can reference specific parts of your workspace directly in your message by mentioning an agent, channel, contact, or label, which helps the copilot target exactly what you mean. Type **/** to insert one of your saved prompts.

## Benefits

- **Seamless iteration**: Move from a question to an answer to a change without leaving the conversation
- **Plain language**: Describe what you want instead of navigating menus and settings
- **Workspace aware**: Answers and actions are scoped to your team's data and configurations
- **Safe by design**: Every configuration change and every piece of delegated work is shown to you for approval before it starts; after that, a goal's messages follow its approval mode
- **Always in context**: Reach your copilot from the home page, the top navigation, a full chat view, Slack, Microsoft Teams, or email

## Credits

Ask Dimedove runs on [credits](/platform/team/credits). Each conversation consumes credits based on the complexity of your queries, so simpler questions cost less than deeper analysis or larger changes. You can track your copilot usage against your team's allowance directly in the product.
