Manage your team members, roles, and permissions.

## Team Members

- **Add Members**: Invite new team members via email
- **Remove Members**: Deactivate or remove team members
- **Role Assignment**: Assign appropriate roles and permissions
- **Status Management**: Track active, inactive, and pending members

## Roles & Permissions

- **Owner**: The team's owner, with full access to all features and settings
- **Admin**: Full access to all features and settings, including inviting users, updating payment, and deleting the team
- **Member**: Manage agents, inbox, and contacts

Some settings can only be changed by an owner or admin, such as turning on agent work in [Goals settings](/platform/goals/settings) and managing other teammates' accounts in [Identity](/platform/team/identity). Only a goal's owner, an admin, or the team owner can change a [goal](/platform/goals/overview).
